# Screen spec: sensor readings, on the rider page and in the instructor's sensor panel

Status: proposed in the pull request for issue #662, for PL9 (plan section 12) and
[ADR 0012](../adr/0012-sensors.md) sections 4, 5 and 6 (PR #660, under review; where this spec and
the ADR differ, the ADR wins until it is accepted). Design system: [system.md](system.md).
Neighbouring specs: [rider.md](rider.md) (the page this adds to), [presenter.md](presenter.md) and
[riders-panel.md](riders-panel.md) (the window this adds to), [room-2d.md](room-2d.md) (the room
screen's strip), [companion.md](companion.md) (the tokens). Reference mock-ups and screenshots:
[mockups/](mockups/), files `sensors-*.html` (rider page, 14 states, generated by
[sensors-pages.js](mockups/sensors-pages.js), styled by [sensors.css](mockups/sensors.css)),
`sensors-presenter-*.html` (presenter window, 13 states, generated by
[sensors-presenter-pages.js](mockups/sensors-presenter-pages.js), styled by
[sensors-presenter.css](mockups/sensors-presenter.css)) and `room-sensors-strip*.html` (room screen,
3 states, styled by [sensors-room.css](mockups/sensors-room.css)), each with `-<w>x<h>.png`.
**Where a mock-up and this text differ, the text wins.**

## Purpose

Show a rider, on their own phone, and the instructor, in the presenter window, **a number and a
gauge** for what a Bluetooth sensor reads, next to what the class plans for that measure. Nothing
more: the readings are **shown, never stored, sent, acted on or compared** (ADR 0012 section 4).
They change no target, no timing and no colour; they are not a score and not advice.

What is settled (issue #662, not reopened here): numbers and gauges only; colour stays the
planned zone and is **never a measured heart-rate zone**; no "adjust by your heart rate" text; no
movement through a scene; readings are never ranked or compared; room-distance readability as in
the design system.

Out of this spec: **riders' readings on the instructor's screen** (`rider_reading`, ADR 0012
section 6, waiting for the owner). It gets its own spec when the owner decides. What it must keep
is already fixed: the presenter window only, never the room screen, join order, no ranking, no
sorting by a value, no bar or colour per rider, nothing with one display (rules 6 and 13).

## What a reading is

| Measure | Row label | Unit | Sensor service (ADR 0012 section 1) | Plan to set it against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heart rate | "Heart rate" | bpm | Heart Rate | The zone's share of the maximum heart rate, in bpm when the person gave one (`hrmaxPct`) |
| Power | "Power" | W | Cycling Power, Fitness Machine | The zone's share of FTP, in watts when the person gave one (`ftpPct`) |
| Cadence | "Cadence" | rpm | Cycling Speed and Cadence, Cycling Power, Fitness Machine | The interval's cadence target, if the contract gives one (gap, below). Otherwise a number only |

- A reading is a **whole number**: heart rate 30 to 250, cadence 0 to 250, power 0 to 2000. A
  value outside its range is not a reading (the pure parser drops it).
- A reading is **held for 3 s at most**. After that the value is **missing**: a dash "–" in grey, no
  tick on the gauge, never the last number. A shown reading changes **at most once a second**, as a
  step (no easing, no counting up).
- **One source per measure.** If two connected sensors give the same measure, the one connected
  first is used and the other's row in the sensors list says "connected, not used". Nothing is
  averaged or compared.
- **Where the unit does not match, there is no gauge.** The gauge needs the plan in the reading's
  own unit: watts for power (the person gave an FTP), bpm for heart rate (a maximum heart rate).
  Without the number the plan is shown as a percentage, the reading is a number alone, and one line
  says where to give it ("Add your FTP in My numbers to see the gauge.").

## The gauge

A line, not a ring (rule 6): a `--stage-raised` track with a 1 px `--stage-line` edge, **the planned
range as a band in the interval's zone colour** (it stands for the zone, so hue is allowed there:
rule 7), and **one white tick** for the reading, 2 px outlined in black so it reads over the band.

- **Scale** (`gauge_calc`, a pure function with table tests): the track runs from one band width
  below the plan to one band width above it; the band width is the plan's width or 15 % of its top
  end, whichever is larger. The band is therefore always the middle third or more. The tick is
  clamped to the track; at an end it stays on the end, and the number says the rest.
- **The tick is white wherever it stands.** No change of colour, weight, shape or words inside or
  outside the band: nothing says "too high", "too low", "in range" or "go up" (rules 8 and 11). The
  gauge shows where the reading is; it never says what to do.
- The gauge has the role `meter` and a text alternative that reads like the row ("Heart rate 142
  bpm, plan 157 to 170").
- It never moves along anything: the tick steps once a second at most (rule 5).

## Part 1. The rider's page

Where it sits: the class view of [rider.md](rider.md), unchanged in order and size. Two things are
added: a second key in the foot row and, once a sensor is connected, readings in the effort block.
The page still sends nothing after `hello`; **sensors send nothing to anyone** (ADR 0012 section 5).
Nothing here is shown before the rider has joined.

### Content, in priority order (a connected sensor)

1. The zone, the countdown (rider.md items 1 and 2), as before.
2. **The readings**, one row per measure the rider's sensors give, each with the plan beside it
   and the gauge: heart rate, power, cadence. The plan rows (power, heart rate, RPE) are unchanged
   and stay for every measure whether or not a sensor gives it.
3. The effort's RPE row, position, next rows, class time, as before.
4. **The sensors key** ("Connect a sensor"; "Sensors" once one is connected), next to **My numbers**.

**Visible at once** (check S4): everything in rider.md plus every reading, every gauge and both
keys, in every connected class state at every supported size. Nothing scrolls.

- **Fitting** (only if the effort rows, the readings and a plate or a hint do not fit): the **next
  rows give way**, three, then two, then one, orientation by orientation, as rider.md's own fitting
  step. The effort rows, the readings, the gauges, a plate and a hint never go. The mock-ups apply

  plate, and at 360 × 640 two with three readings and one in the worst case.

### The sensors key and its sheet

**Connect a sensor** starts the browser's own device list (the page cannot draw or restyle it; ADR
0012 section 5). The list is asked for only on this press, never by itself, and asks only for the
four services of ADR 0012 section 1. **Sensors** opens a sheet over the view (as My numbers does;
the view goes on underneath) with each connected device by its own name, its state **in words and
by a marker** (filled square: connected; dashed square: lost), **Disconnect** (or **Reconnect**
when lost), **Connect another sensor**, and **Done**. The sheet scrolls down if it must; it is a
form for the checks. Closing the browser's list without choosing is not a failure and says nothing.

The page **disconnects every sensor** when the class ends, when the rider is removed and when the
page closes. It never reconnects by itself: a lost sensor stays lost until the rider presses
**Reconnect** (a default, reversible; it keeps "connect on a user's tap", ADR 0012 section 3).

permission** (the "Could not connect" plate).

### Motion

A reading and its tick change in a **step, at most once a second**. Plates come and go in
`--m-state` (300 ms). No animation on a key, no flash, nothing that follows a reading. With
`prefers-reduced-motion` the plates appear at once; nothing else changes, since nothing else moves.

### Wording

Sentence case, plain words, one fact a line (presenter.md). No exclamation marks, no generated text.

| Where | Words |
|---|---|
| Keys | "Connect a sensor", "Sensors", "Choosing…", "Reconnect", "Disconnect", "Connect another sensor", "Done" |
| Plates | "Choose your sensor", "Sensor lost", "Could not connect" |
| Lines | "Add your FTP in My numbers to see the gauge.", "No heart rate from your strap.", "Readings stay on this phone. Nothing is sent, saved or compared." |
| States in the sheet | "Heart rate, connected", "Power and cadence, connected", "Heart rate, lost", "connected, not used" |
| Never | "too high", "too low", "in range", "target heart rate zone", "adjust", "push", "ease off", any word that tells the rider what to do |

## Part 2. The instructor's sensor panel

**The presenter window, on the second display** ([presenter.md](presenter.md)); with one display the
presenter window lies behind the room window and the panel is reached there (class-list.md, "Where
the room window goes"). It follows the riders panel's pattern: a **row** at the foot of the right
column and a **drawer**. The row sits **above the riders row**; the two are the last things in the
column. Only one drawer is open at a time (opening one closes the other); the riders row and its
count stay in view while the sensors drawer is open. Sizes are in pixels (the presenter is read at
arm's length), the window at 1000 × 720 or larger. The instructor's own numbers and readings are
the instructor's: they live in the window's memory, are never sent, never written to the class
history or a log, and clear when the window closes.

### The row (closed)

```
+-------------------------------+
| Sensors  Connected      [Show]|   head, at least 44 px
| 142 bpm   238 W   92 rpm      |   the live numbers, white
|-------------------------------|
| 12 Riders          [Show list]|
+-------------------------------+
```

- The head: "Sensors" (Atkinson 700, 17 px), the state in words (Atkinson 400, 15 px, `--light-2`) and
  **Show** (at least 44 px high). The line below: up to three readings in the order heart rate,
  power, cadence, each the number (Big Shoulders 800, **26 px**) and its unit (15 px, `--light-2`),
  22 px apart. A missing reading is a grey dash.
- The row is at most **100 px** high, so with the resync notice showing the whole closed column
  still fits at 1000 × 720 (the mock-ups show it).

### The drawer (open)

The same place and rules as the riders drawer: under the status lines and the notice, over the next
song and the controls, never covering the notice's Undo.

| Part | Content | Scrolls? |
|---|---|---|
| Head | "Sensors", the state, **Hide** | No |
| Readings | Per measure: label (15 px) and plan ("Plan 157–170 bpm", 15 px) on one line; under it the gauge (10 px high, the free width) and the reading (Big Shoulders 800, **30 px**) with its unit | No |
| Room screen | One display only: the switch **Show on the room screen**, its state in words ("Off. S turns it on" / "On. Covers the score. S turns it off"). At least 44 px high | No |
| List | Each device (its own name, up to two lines, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, then an ellipsis; its state in words and by a marker; **Disconnect** or **Reconnect**), **Connect another sensor**, "Your FTP, in watts" and "Your max heart rate, in bpm" (two fields, 44 px high, whole numbers 50 to 600 and 80 to 230, as the riders'), the note "Readings stay on this computer. Nothing is sent, saved or compared." | Yes, inside the drawer |

At 1000 × 720 with the notice showing, the head, the readings and the room switch show with no
scroll and the first device row shows; with more, the list scrolls. **Without the instructor's FTP
and maximum heart rate** the plan is a percentage, the readings are numbers with no gauge, and a
line says where to type them ([no numbers](mockups/sensors-presenter-no-numbers-1000x720.png)). The
fields are the only inputs; they are not remembered between runs (a default, reversible: rule 11
keeps anything from a past session out of the screen).

### Choosing

**Connect a sensor** (in the row, the drawer or the list) opens, in the same list area, **the app's
own list of nearby devices** (ADR 0012 section 6: the main process answers the chooser): a line
"Looking for sensors. Wake yours.", each device that offers one of the four services by the name it
gives and the service in words, **Connect** on each, and **Cancel**. Nothing is chosen by itself and
nothing is listed that offers no allowed service. A failed connection shows one line ("Could not
connect to Polar H10 A1B2C3. Check that it is awake.") and keeps the list.

### With one display: the room screen's strip (off by default)

With one display the room window fills the display and hides the presenter window, so the
instructor's own readings can be shown **on the room screen**, which the riders see. That is the
instructor's choice and it is **off by default, every class** (never remembered).

- **Turn on or off:** the key **S** in the room window, or the switch in the drawer above. **S is
  one more row of room-2d.md's keyboard table**: it does nothing when there are two displays (the
  room screen never shows readings then), when the panel is unavailable, or when no sensor is
  connected (the switch then says "Connect a sensor first" and is off). It does not show the band.
  No sixth button is added to the control band.
- **Where:** the strip lies **exactly over the score's box** (79.5 vh and 15.5 vh high; 83 vh and
  12 vh at 3:2 and narrower) the screen's full width (the cells keep the safe edges), and **covers nothing else**. The score is
  hidden under it while it shows, the cost the instructor accepts when turning it on. The control
  band covers it as it covers the score (room-2d.md, C10). The join sheet ends above it.
- **Content:** three cells in one row, heart rate, power, cadence. Each: the label and the plan
  ("Plan 157–170", in grey) stacked, the reading (`--t-value`) with its unit (`--t-minor`), and the
  gauge under them, as wide as they are. A missing reading
  is a dash; a cell with no sensor is not drawn. No title, no rider, no count: **only the
  instructor's own sensors, never a rider's reading** (rule 13).
- Plan and gauge follow the part-2 rule: with no instructor's numbers the strip shows the readings
  alone.
- **Motion:** the numbers and the tick change in a step at most once a second, as a step of the
  room's flash budget (room-2d.md, "Motion"): no more than the 500 ms between marks it already allows.

## Measurable criteria (stated as numbers)

[shoot.js](mockups/shoot.js) and [measure.js](mockups/measure.js) run these on every `sensors-*` and
`room-sensors-*` shot (`ONLY=sensors- node shoot.js`; the rider and presenter checks are
[rider-checks.js](mockups/rider-checks.js), whose list of the page's own actions gains
`sensors`, `sensors_add`, `reconnect_sensor` and `disconnect_sensor`, none of which sends a command
or touches equipment). The real screens' CI screenshots are held to the same numbers against the
fake `SensorSource`; every floored text stays at least 5 % over its floor there.

| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| S1. Text height | Cap height as a share of the **viewport height**, portrait / landscape, with a floor in CSS pixels. **Rider page**: a reading **4.6 % / 7.4 %, 29 px** (new tier `read`, [companion-checks.js](mockups/companion-checks.js)); planned values 3.4 % / 6 %, 21 px; units, plates, keys, device names 1.9 % / 3.4 %, 12.5 px; labels, hints and state words 1.5 % / 2.7 %, 10 px. **Presenter window**, in pixels: a reading **17.5 px** (26 px type; 30 px in the drawer, 21 px), labels, units, state words 10.5 px, controls and names 11.8 px. **Room strip**, as room-2d.md C1: a reading (`--t-value`) at least **5.5 %**, its unit 3 %, labels and plan 2 % |
| S2. Contrast | At least **7 : 1** for every text. The gauge, not text: the tick against the track and the band against the track at least **3 : 1**; the tick carries a 2 px black outline so it reads over the band |
| S3. No collisions | No two texts overlap; nothing wraps beyond its slot or is cut, except the designed ellipsis (a device name over two lines) |
| S4. Visible at once | Rider: every reading, every gauge, both keys, and everything of rider.md's R11. Presenter: the closed row and the notice's Undo; the drawer's head, readings and room switch (the list scrolls). Room strip: all three cells |
| S5. Hue | Hue only in a gauge's band (a zone element); readings, ticks, plates, switches, devices and every state are white, grey and black (measure.js "hue outside a zone") |
| S6. Touch targets | Rider: every key at least **64 x 64 px**, at least **12 px** apart (R4). Presenter: every button at least **44 px** high |
| S7. One screen | Rider class states: no scroll in either direction. The sheets: no sideways scroll. Presenter: the list scrolls inside the drawer, nothing else does |
| S8. Motion | With reduced motion no transition or animation lasts longer than 0 (R9); a shown number changes at most once a second |
| S9. Words | The shown text contains none of the words of "Never" in the wording table (a test over the strings of the screens' catalogue) |

## Guards the build adds

In addition to ADR 0012 sections 1 and 5 (the UUID allowlist, no write call, one file names
`navigator.bluetooth`): **no zone colour from a measurement** (a test that the colour token of a
reading, a tick and a gauge's track is never a zone token, and that the tick's token is the same
inside and outside the band); **no word of advice** (S9); **nothing stored or sent** (no
`localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, request or message holds a reading; the page's only message after
joining is still `hello`); **the room strip never shows a rider's reading** (a test that the
room window's channel carries only the instructor's own readings, and none when there are two
displays).

## What the screens never show or do (plan section 3)

| Rule | How this spec keeps it |
|---|---|
| 1. A finished class only | Readings change nothing about the class: no edit, no skipped interval, no suggested change |
| 2. Software only, no equipment | Sensors are read: connect and switch on notifications, nothing more. No control of resistance, speed, incline or a brake; no control point is named (ADR 0012 section 1); no button of these screens sends a command |
| 3. Spotify's music stays in Spotify's app | No audio, no song |
| 4. No road, ribbon, track, terrain or world | The gauge is a straight line with a band; it is not travelled along |
| 5. No viewpoint or rider moving through a scene | A number and a gauge, changing in a step at most once a second; nothing moves because of a reading |
| 6. None of MOWL's look | No ring, no row of class bars, no circle or bar per rider: one straight gauge for one's own reading, never for several riders; our palette |
| 7. Colour means the planned zone | The only hue is a gauge's band, which is the planned zone; a reading, a tick and every state are white, grey or black; **no measured heart-rate zone** anywhere |
| 8. No hand-position icons, no "adjust by your heart rate" | No icon; no advice; the "Never" words are tested (S9) |
| 9. Own names for load figures | "Power" in watts and "Cadence" in rpm are readings, not load figures; no TSS, normalised power or intensity factor |
| 10. Cues are designed text | No cue and no generated or spoken text; every string is fixed |
| 11. Nothing from past sessions, no intensity changes from performance | Readings live in memory only, are never saved and never change a target, a time or a colour; "actual against planned" afterwards is later work with its own ADR |
| 12. No camera | Bluetooth only; no camera |
| 13. Rider data one way | A rider's readings stay on that phone; this spec sends none and shows none of anyone else's. The instructor's own readings are the instructor's; the room strip shows only those, off by default; riders' readings on the presenter are out of this spec, with their constraints recorded above |
| 14. Join code is a session id only | No code or token is shown or used by these screens |
| 15. No team games | No ranking, sorting by a reading, comparison, points or "ride together" language |

## Contract gaps, defaults and open questions

- **Cadence has no plan in `rider_state` today** (`effort` carries `ftpPct`, `hrmaxPct`, `rpe`). The
  mock-ups show cadence as a number with no gauge. If the owner or the contract adds a cadence
  range per interval (the class file has one for the room screen), the gauge appears with the same
  rules; a contract-lane change.
- **The instructor's FTP and maximum heart rate** have no home in the player yet; this spec types
  them in the drawer and keeps them in the window's memory only. Reversible.
- **One display, the room strip** is a default made here (the issue says "off by default"):
  S and the drawer's switch, the score's box, not remembered. It adds a row to room-2d.md's keyboard
  table and replaces its rule 5 note "no sensor data is shown in this version". A sixth band
  control is not proposed (the owner's five).
- **Reconnect by tap only** and **first connected source wins** are defaults, reversible.
- **Reading ranges** (heart rate 30 to 250, cadence 0 to 250, power 0 to 2000) are defaults of the
  pure parser; a change is a design pull request.
- **Riders' readings on the presenter** wait for the owner's decision in ADR 0012 section 6.
