# Screen spec: the web player page `/play`

Status: proposed for issue #390, the design of [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-the-player-as-a-web-app.md)
section 5. Design system: [system.md](system.md). It sets a new direction only for phone and
portrait sizes: everything else is the room screen of [room-2d.md](room-2d.md) and its control
band, and the picker is a page of [site.md](site.md). Where this spec says nothing, those specs
hold. Reference mock-ups and screenshots: [mockups/](mockups/), files `play-*.html` (19 mock-ups,
generated by [play-pages.js](mockups/play-pages.js) from [play-kit.js](mockups/play-kit.js) and
[site-kit.js](mockups/site-kit.js), laid out by [play.css](mockups/play.css) over
[room.css](mockups/room.css) and [controls.css](mockups/controls.css)) and `play-*-<size>.png`.
**Where a mock-up and this text differ, the text wins.**

## Purpose

A page of the website that runs one class from the instructor's library on the manual clock,
with no computer and no installed player: on a tablet on a stand or on the handlebar, on a phone
on the handlebar, or on a tablet or laptop connected to the studio's screen. It is the room
screen, laid out for the screen it is on. The instructor starts the music in its own app and
presses Start with it (ADR 0008 section 1); from then on the page needs no network.

It does three things and nothing else:

- **Pick a class**: the signed-in library, as the website lists it.
- **Show it**: the room screen's information, the same at every size.
- **Move through it**: Start, Pause, Resume, Previous, Next and End, as touch targets, and the
  band's keys for a keyboard or a presentation clicker.

There is no music link, no audio, no live session, no class code, no sensor and no equipment
(ADR 0008 section 1). Nothing is stored in the browser. A reload loses the class.

## Content, in priority order

The room screen's list (room-2d.md, "Content, in priority order"), unchanged:

1. **The zone**: the gel, with the zone as number, name and colour, and its targets.
2. **Time left in the interval**: the countdown.
3. **The cue.**
4. **Cadence and position**, and the song's BPM.
5. **Next**: the next interval's zone (chip and name), length, cadence and position.
6. **The score**: the class as a strip under a fixed "now".
7. **Class time left** and the **link slot** (here always the manual clock, or one of the page's
   two notices below).
8. **Class time done** and the **segment name** (dropped in portrait, see below).

What this page adds is only the band (always the touch way in), two notices in the link slot
("Screen may turn off", "Reloaded: back to the start") and the picker.

### Visible at once

At every size, without scrolling:

- **While the class runs** (running, paused, End asked): the zone number and name, class time
  left, the link slot, the countdown, cadence, position and BPM, the cue, the next row, and the
  score or the band over it.
- **Before the start and on "Class Complete"**: the gel (zone number and name, or "Class
  Complete"), the link slot, the class title, its facts, the time in each zone, and the band with
  Start or Close class.

The page check P6 tests these lists on every mock-up.

## Sizes

Three layouts, chosen from the viewport alone:

| Layout | When | Reference sizes | Smallest checked | Floors (P1) | Score window |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Room** | wider than tall, at least 720 lines | 1024×768 (a tablet on its side) | 1024×768 | the room's, C1 | 6 minutes |
| **Small landscape** | wider than tall, under 720 lines | 844×390 (a phone on its side) | 640×360 | the companion's landscape, K1 | 6 minutes |
| **Portrait** | as tall as wide or taller | 390×844 (a phone), 768×1024 (a tablet) | 360×640 | the companion's portrait, K1 | 3 minutes |

- **Room** is the room screen's own layout for its size (room-2d.md, "Layout"): at 1024×768 its
  narrow layout. A laptop or a screen at 1280×720 and up is the room screen exactly as room-2d.md
  draws it. The only change is in the band: buttons at least 64 × 64 CSS px and at least 12 px
  apart (P4), which at 1024×768 widens the gap from 0.8 vw (8 px) to 12 px.
- **Small landscape** and **portrait** are new and apply only on this page. The room window of
  the player and its 480×270 preview in the presenter never use them: they are scoped to the
  page (in the mock-ups, `body.play`).
- **Between 1:1 and 4:3** (a tablet in split view): portrait up to 1:1, then the landscape
  layouts. Sizes between 3:4 and 4:3 are not checked, like a room window shrunk by hand.
- **The browser's bars.** Every size is the visible area, not the device: with an iPhone's bars
  showing, a 390×844 phone has about 390×664 (the full-screen-not-available shot). The product
  sizes in `dvh` (the dynamic viewport) and adds `env(safe-area-inset-*)` to the paddings
  (`viewport-fit=cover`), so nothing sits under a notch, a rounded corner or the home bar.
- Sizes are shares of the viewport (`vh`, `vw`), as on the room screen. Touch targets are in CSS
  px, the one exception.

### The score on small screens

The score is the room's: the class as a strip, "now" fixed at 25 vw, the past darkened, blocks
in their zone colour with the number and, only if it fits whole, the name; songs below with title,
artist and BPM if they fit; moments and the end mark. What changes is the time it shows:

- **Landscape** (room and small landscape): 6 minutes across, 0.28 vw a second, as on the room
  screen: 90 s behind "now", 4:30 ahead.
- **Portrait**: **3 minutes** across, 0.56 vw a second: 45 s behind, 2:15 ahead. At 390 px a
  minute is 130 px wide, where 6 minutes would leave a 30-second interval 16 px wide.
- A block, clip or moment that begins right of the window is not drawn; one that runs past the
  right safe edge keeps its labels inside the screen (the room's rule). A block that began in the
  past keeps its number just right of "now".
- The sliver rule (room-2d.md: no number in a block that has under 3 vw left ahead of "now") and
  `NUMBER_FITS_UNTIL` (91 vw) are per layout: in portrait the sliver threshold is
  max(3 vw, 1.1 em of the block number), so a number is never squeezed into a sliver narrower than
  itself.
- The score's box clips the strip: the page never scrolls sideways (P6).

### Fitting text

The room's rules (room-2d.md, "Fitting text"), with these changes for the two new layouts:

- **Zone name on the gel**: one or two lines at value; a name that needs more becomes minor and
  ends in an ellipsis after **two** lines (three on the room screen), so the cue keeps its height.
  A two-line name beside three targets is set at minor too.
- **Cue**: up to 120 characters, never cut and never scrolled. Up to 60 characters at text,
  longer at textmin. Portrait: the cue takes all the height left, which holds a 120-character cue
  with long words at 360×640 and at 390×844 beside the longest gel ([worst](mockups/play-worst-390x844.png)).
  Small landscape: two lines at text or three at textmin.
- **Next row**: in portrait the name ends in an ellipsis on the first line, and cadence and
  position move to a second line; the chip and the length never drop.
- **Class title**: one line, then an ellipsis (the picker shows it whole).
- **Time in each zone**: one line per zone at every phone size.
- **Rail**: in portrait, only the class time left and the link slot. Done and the segment name are
  dropped (both are on the room screen; done is the class length minus what is left).

## Controls and keys

The band of room-2d.md, "Controls on the room screen", with the page's own rules:

- **Buttons**: Previous ("Interval"), Start / Pause / Resume, Next ("Interval"), and End, at the
  right. On "Class Complete", one button, "Close class" with "Back to the class list", in End's
  place. Words, never icons; white, grey and black only.
- **End and its guard**: End opens "End the class?" with **"End class"** ("Back to the class
  list") and **"Keep going"**, which has the focus and sits where End was. No answer in 8 s, Esc
  or a tap outside the band: "Keep going". In portrait the question sits on its own line above
  the two buttons, because one row would not fit.
- **End class** and **Close class** drop the class from memory and show the picker (`/play`,
  fragment cleared). That is the first request since Start (ADR 0008 section 2).
- **Touch**: a tap while the band is hidden shows it and presses nothing. A tap on a button while
  it is shown presses it. No swipe, long press or double tap does anything. Every button is at
  least **64 × 64 CSS px** and at least **12 px** from the next (P4), at every size.
- **Shown and hidden**: as on the room screen with one display. Pinned before the start (Start)
  and on "Class Complete" (Close class); shown on a tap, Tab or End; hidden 3 s after the last
  input; held by keyboard focus and by the End question.
- **No Undo, no Follow the music, no Manual clock**: the page has only the manual clock (ADR 0008
  section 5). No class code, no QR code, no Phone remote.

**Keys** (a tablet with a keyboard, a presentation clicker): the room screen's table
(room-2d.md, "Keyboard") minus the keys that have no meaning here.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Space | Start, Pause or Resume: what the main button shows |
| Right arrow, Page Down | Next |
| Left arrow, Page Up | Previous |
| Shift+Right, Shift+Left | Forward 5 s, Back 5 s on the manual clock |
| End | Opens the End question |
| F5, Shift+F5 | Start, only before the start; after it, nothing |
| B, . (period) | Nothing: the screen never goes blank |
| Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter | Into the band, through it, press |
| Esc | Closes the End question ("Keep going"); otherwise hides the band at once. In full screen the browser also leaves full screen on Esc; the page says nothing about it |

Not here: Ctrl+Z (there is no resync to undo), F (no music to follow), J (no join sheet). While
the End question is open, Space, the arrows, Page Up, Page Down, F5 and Shift+F5 do nothing.

**Keep-awake** (ADR 0008 section 5): the Screen Wake Lock is asked for at Start, held through
pauses, released at "Class Complete", at End class and when the page is hidden, and asked for
again when the page is shown during a class.

**Full screen**: Start asks for full screen where `document.fullscreenEnabled` (Start is a tap,
which the Fullscreen API needs). Resume does not ask again: leaving full screen is the person's
choice. Where it is not available, nothing is said. The layout is the same in and out of full
screen; only the size changes.

**Offline during a class** changes nothing on the screen (ADR 0008 section 2): there is no state
for it.

## Motion and reduced motion

The room screen's motion (room-2d.md, "Motion and reduced motion"), without the beat dot (no
music link): the gel's colour crosses in 700 ms at an interval change with the white edge for
160 ms, the score moves continuously, and the band appears and fades in 300 ms. With
`prefers-reduced-motion`: colour changes are instant, no flash, the score steps once a second,
and the band is there and gone at once. Nothing repeats; nothing relies on motion to be
understood. Entering or leaving full screen is the browser's own and is not animated by the page.

## What the page never shows or does (plan section 3)
